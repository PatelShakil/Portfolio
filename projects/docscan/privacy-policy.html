Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31st July, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

🔒 100% Offline & On-Device Privacy Commitment: DOCSCAN processes all documents, image filters, camera feeds, and PDF exports entirely on your device. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal documents or user data with external servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DOCSCAN ("the Application"), developed by MuhammadShakil Patel (TechSavvy Solution), handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices. We believe your private documents belong strictly to you.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We practice zero-data collection:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or IP addresses.
  • No Document Uploads: All photos captured or imported into DOCSCAN remain stored locally on your device's internal application storage.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: DOCSCAN does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral advertisement SDKs.

3. Application Permissions

To provide core document scanning capabilities, DOCSCAN requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): Required exclusively for real-time document frame detection, auto-capture, and photo scanning inside the viewfinder. Camera streams are analyzed live in device memory and are never uploaded or recorded externally.
  • Storage / Media Access: Required to allow users to import existing image files from their gallery and export generated PDF or ZIP files to local device storage.

4. Third-Party Libraries

DOCSCAN utilizes open-source computer vision libraries (OpenCV) and Android Jetpack libraries solely for local image processing, layout rendering, and edge detection. None of these libraries transmit data over the network.

5. Security

Because all document data and PDF files reside exclusively on your physical device, the security of your scanned files relies on your Android device’s built-in security features, such as screen lock, encryption, and biometric authentication.

6. Children's Privacy

DOCSCAN does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
